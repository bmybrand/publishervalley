Our Path to Publication

Inside the Making of a Picture Book

A picture book is won in the details. Here’s how we make self-publishing a children's picture book manageable.

01 / Illustration

Defining the Art Direction

Book publishing for kids is not easy; they notice more than we give them credit for. For them, illustration is not just decoration sitting beside the words. It makes them curious about turning page after page. We choose the visual direction around the story so every spread feels like it belongs.

We don't jump straight into finished illustrations. First, we work on rough page-by-page sketches to plan the action and page turns. Then we work on the character designs, choosing between gouache and a mixed approach based on the story. Then we prepare the final illustrations with the correct resolution.

02 / Editing

Shaping the Story

A picture book does not give weak writing anywhere to hide. You may have only a few hundred words to move a child through an entire story. So, every line has to earn its place. We just aim for one thing when editing: “nothing should interrupt the moment when a child wants to turn the page”.

Our children's picture book editors first look at the story as a whole before editing a phrase. Then we proceed to tighten the pace and make sure every sentence is appropriate for the age group. We also check how the words work with the artwork.

03 / Formatting

Making Every Page Fit

A picture book can have great artwork and still end up looking wrong when the pages come together. That happens when text sits too close to the edge or important details disappear into the binding. That’s why we build the layout around the artwork to give every spread its own space.

Whether it’s an illustrated book or a simple written story, formatting follows one rule: imagine how it will look after printing. We handle the trim size, margins, and bleed before the book goes to print. We make sure nothing important gets swallowed by the spine after you get a published book in hand.

04 / eBook Production

Bringing the Pages to Digital

A picture eBook isn't formatted like a regular novel. The text and the illustrations need to stay together because moving either one can mess with the whole page. We build each screen around the original spread so the story’s artwork and words stay where it was meant to be.

Our fixed-layout ebook formatting service also includes the file preparation for each platform. That includes image quality, page dimensions, text placement, and metadata. Our team makes sure that when you open the eBook on a supported device, you see the same story you approved in print.

One formatting mistake can cost more than time.

05 / Printing

Selecting Your Print Format

Picture books get opened wide and are read again and again. That's why printing the book is the most crucial step of the journey. We offer two options in them. First, Hardcover children’s book printing gives you a sturdier finish. Second, a paperback that keeps the price lower and makes reprints easier.

Then there is the paper itself. We also pay attention to the weight and finish because they can change how your colors look and how pages feel. We help you choose the combination that fits the story, audience, and budget.

06 / Publishing & Distribution

Getting the Book Into the World

A finished book sitting in your files is not a published book. We prepare the book for the platforms where it needs to live, including Amazon KDP and IngramSpark. Then set up the files and publishing details around each one.

Then we work on the details people rarely think about until they become a problem. We set the right categories, keywords, and also the metadata so your book lands in the right searches. The intention is to give it somewhere to be found.

FAQs

The Questions That Come Before Publication

How Much Does It Cost to Publish a Children’s Picture Book?

+

There is no single price because every book needs different work. Your cost depends on the services you need and how much of the process you hand over to us.

Do I Need an Illustrator for My Children’s Picture Book?

+

If your story relies on artwork, then yes, the illustrations carry a big part of the experience. We offer online book publishing for kids that can help shape the visual direction of your story.

How Long Does It Take to Publish a Children’s Picture Book?

+

There is no magic deadline for a good picture book. Editing, illustration, and revisions can take time, but we build the process around your time frame.

What Should the Standard Children's Picture Book Size Be?

+

Square and portrait formats are common, but the right size depends on your artwork. We help choose a format that works for the final print.

Can I Publish My Picture Book on Amazon KDP and IngramSpark?

+

Yes. We can prepare your book for platforms such as Amazon KDP and IngramSpark so you can reach readers through more than one publishing route.

Do I Need an ISBN for My Children’s Book?

+

An ISBN identifies your book and its edition in the publishing system. We also offer children’s book ISBN services to help you sort out the publishing details before your book goes live.
