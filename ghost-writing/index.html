Voice Adaptation

How We Make the Writing Sound Like You

The biggest concern clients come to us with is always: "Will this still sound like me?" We make sure the answer is yes. From your first conversation to the final page, we learn your voice and keep it at the heart of the book.

01

Voice Interview & Analysis

We start with a real conversation and look at anything you have already written. For example, your emails, notes, and transcripts to understand how you naturally speak and write.

02

Personal Style Guide

Then, the writer creates a personal style guide based upon your tone, word choices, and the phrases that sound like you. This becomes the guide for the entire project.

03

Calibration Chapter

Before moving ahead with the full manuscript, we create a sample chapter. You tell us what feels okay and what needs a bit of changing until the writing feels like it came from you.

04

Ongoing Refinement

As the manuscript grows, we stay in touch and use your feedback to keep the voice consistent. No chapters that feel like they came from a different writer.

Our Approach

A Clear Process Built for Better Books

We always follow a roadmap for every service we offer, and ghostwriting is no exception. Every writer follows the same method to ensure a smooth process for both the client and the team.

01

Consultation

We begin with a conversation about your book. Gathering up your ideas and what you want the finished book to achieve.

02

Story Planning

Before writing starts, we create your book outline and sum up the research needed to support it. You review the direction before we move forward.

03

Project Setup

Once the plan is approved, your writer gets everything needed to move ahead. We organize your materials, confirm the scope, and set the writing schedule.

04

Manuscript Development

The assigned ghostwriter then develops the manuscript chapter by chapter. Through regular check-ins, we try to keep you involved and make sure the book stays on track.

05

Editorial Refinement

Once the manuscript is complete, it undergoes editing and proofreading. We refine the structure and catch the tiny details before delivery.

06

Publishing Preparation

You receive your completed manuscript ready for its next chapter. If you need it, we can also help with book cover design, formatting, and publishing.

FAQs

The Questions Authors Ask First

We have some of the most frequently asked questions from our clients.

How Much Does Ghostwriting Cost?
The cost is up to your book's length, genre, and how much research is required. Once we know your project, we can give you a clear quote about the cost.
Can A Ghostwriter Write In My Voice?
Absolutely. We spend time learning how you speak and express your ideas. Your existing writing helps your ghostwriter to adapt a writing voice identical to yours.
Do I Own The Book After It Is Written?
Yes. Ownership and rights are set out in your agreement. With a full ghostwriting arrangement, you can retain the rights to your manuscript, including its future use and royalties.
Can I Hire A Ghostwriter If I Only Have An Idea?
Surely. You do not need a finished manuscript to get started. We can work from rough notes; even recordings or unfinished chapters would work to shape it into a complete book.
How Long Does It Take To Ghostwrite A Book?
Every book has its own timeframe. Length and your feedback play a part. We set a clear schedule at the beginning so you know when you can expect the final book.
Will I Be Involved In The Writing Process?
Yes. You stay involved as much as you want. We can work with you through consultations and revisions, while your writer handles the heavy lifting.
Is Ghostwriting Confidential?
Yes. Your project is protected by a signed NDA and confidentiality terms. Your ideas and manuscript stay private throughout the process.
How Do I Choose The Right Ghostwriter?
The right ghostwriter should understand your kind of book and be able to work comfortably with your voice, ideas, and goals.
