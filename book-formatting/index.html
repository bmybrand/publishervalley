Genre Expertise

Every Genre Demands a Different Kind of Page

Being in this industry for more than 10 years has taught us so many things, with the main one being "no two books are ever formatted the same." So we start formatting a book for print around the way its genre is meant to be read.

Storytelling

Fiction Book Formatting

We focus more on the chapter openings, how scene breaks and typography are shaped to keep the story moving without getting in its way.

Informational

Nonfiction Book Formatting

Nonfiction is more about observing headings, footnotes, and callouts. Making certain they are arranged to keep information clear without crowding the page.

Poetic

Poetry Book Formatting

Poetry lives in its spacing. So, we work to protect the line breaks, stanzas, and white space so the page never swallows the poem.

Illustrated

Children's Book Formatting

In children's book layout and formatting, the words and illustrations need to share the page without competing. We craft layouts around both.

Devotional

Christian Book Formatting

Our Christian book formatting services involve paying close attention to the devotionals and study material, in order to get a clear layout that keeps every page easy to follow.

Visual

Comic Book Formatting

Every panel of a comic has a place. Bleed, gutters, and safe areas are handled so artwork and speech stay where they belong.

Reference

Dictionary & Reference Formatting

Dense information needs order. Our team of experts makes sure the entries, glossaries, and cross-references are laid out for quick reading.

Professional

Memoir & Business Book Formatting

A memoir should feel personal. Whereas a business book should feel clear. Neither needs to look like it came from the same template.

The Formatting Process

The Work Behind a Properly Formatted Book

01

Format Assessment

Before the pages are touched, we look at what is already there. We review the manuscript and the publishing platform so the formatting starts on solid ground.

02

Typography & Layout Direction

Every book needs its own visual rhythm. We choose the typeface and page structure to follow that and give your manuscript a consistent look.

03

Complete Interior Formatting

This is where the manuscript finally starts looking like a book. We build the front matter, chapter openings, and back matter into one finished interior.

04

Proofing & Layout Review

A page can look fine until you actually inspect it. We give it another round of inspection to find any missed awkward breaks or bad hyphenation before they reach the final file.

05

Publishing Handoff

Once everything is in place, we prep the files for your chosen platform. You receive the finished versions along with your source files.

FAQs

Got Questions About Book Formatting?

Book formatting comes with plenty of questions. Here are the ones the authors ask us most.

How much does book formatting cost?
It's up to the type of formatting you choose. Send us your manuscript, and we'll tell you exactly what it needs and what it will cost.
How long does book formatting take?
Most standard projects take approximately 5–7 business days. If you need it sooner, then we also have options for tighter deadlines.
Can you format my book for major publishing platforms?
Yes. We prepare your files around each platform's requirements, so the formatting headache is off your shoulders.
Can you format for both print and ebook versions?
Yes. Print and ebook files follow different rules. We have a team who format each one for the way your readers will actually see it.
What file should I send for formatting?
A Word document is usually all we need. If you have your manuscript in Google Docs or another format, don't worry, just send it over, and we'll take it from there.
Can you format books with images or illustrations?
Of course. We format children's books, comics, poetry, and other image-heavy projects where every element needs its place.
Can you format my book for IngramSpark and Lulu?
Absolutely. We prepare your files for Lulu and other publishing platforms based on their specific requirements.
Can you format my book if I have never published before?
Sure. You do not need to know the technical side of book formatting. Send us your manuscript, and we'll guide you through the process.
