Technical Quality Control

Your Audiobook, Fully Produced and ACX-Ready

Good narration is only half the job. Your audiobook also needs correct levels and properly prepared files. We check those details before delivery so you are not left fixing technical problems at the finish line.

Format

File & Codec

192 kbps constant bit-rate MP3, 44.1kHz sample rate, mono or stereo depending on retailer, delivered chapter by chapter.

Loudness

RMS, Peak & Noise Floor

RMS levels between -23dB and -18dB, peaks no higher than -3dB, and a room-tone noise floor at or below -60dB RMS.

Structure

Opening & Closing Credits

Retailer-compliant title and credit reads, chapter naming conventions, and required room tone at the head and tail of every file.

Audio Post-Production

Turning Raw Narration Into Retail-Ready Audio

Raw recordings need to have a quick cleanup before they are ready for listeners. Through our best audiobook service, you can balance the audio and check every chapter against the required technical standards.

01

Audio Cleanup

We remove mouth clicks, unwanted breaths, and other distractions without taking away the life out of the performance.

Manuscript Proofing

FAQs

Audiobook Questions, Answered

How Do I Publish My Audiobook on Audible or ACX?
You need more than a finished recording. The files have to meet the platform's technical requirements. We handle all of that to help you with the upload process.
How Do I Choose the Right Audiobook Narrator?
Start with the story. A narrator should fit the genre, rather than simply have a "good voice." We provide audition samples too, so you can hear the options before making the call.
Can the Author Narrate Their Own Audiobook?
Yes. If your voice fits the book, give it a go. We can then handle the editing, mastering, and technical side afterwards.
Can Text-To-Speech Audiobook Production Meet ACX and Audible Standards?
Audio quality is only part of the equation. Platform rules also matter. We recommend checking the latest ACX and Audible policies before using text-to-speech.
How Does ACX Royalty Share And Monetization Work?
ACX offers different ways to pay for and monetize audiobook production. Authors can pay production costs upfront or use eligible royalty-sharing arrangements.
Are You An Affordable Audiobook Production Option For Indie And Self-Published Authors?
Yes. We keep production flexible so you pay for what your audiobook actually needs. No bloated package just because you're an indie author.
What Audiobook Production And Distribution Standards Do You Follow?
We check your audio against the requirements of your target platforms. Files are edited, mastered, and prepared for delivery.
