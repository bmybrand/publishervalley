How much do book marketing services cost?

What's included in your book promotion packages?
Most packages combine Amazon or social ads, review generation, and email or press outreach. Every package is built around your genre and goals rather than a one-size-fits-all bundle.
Do you offer affordable book marketing services for self-published authors?
Yes. Our self-publishing plans are built with independent budgets in mind, with entry-level packages that can scale up as your book earns momentum.
Can you run Amazon book advertising and KDP campaigns?
Yes Put Your Marketing Budget to Better Use Amazon and KDP marketing is one of our most requested services. We manage Sponsored Products, category positioning, A+ Content and review generation.
