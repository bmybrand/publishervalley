FAQs

A Few Things Authors Ask Us First

What's the Difference Between Editing and Proofreading?
Editing improves the writing itself. Proofreading, on the other hand, is the final check for typos and little mistakes before you publish.
How Much Does Book Proofreading Cost?
It's all up to the final word count and how much work the manuscript requires. Send it over, and we'll give you a clear quote.
How Long Will It Take to Proofread My Book?
That depends on the length and deadline. Do you need it quickly? We also have rush and same-day options available.
Can I See a Sample Before I Hire You?
Absolutely. Send us your first 1,000 words, and we'll show you how we approach your pages before you commit.
Will You Change the Way I Write?
No. Your voice stays yours. We just fix the mistakes without changing your writing at all.
Will My Manuscript Stay Private?
Yes. Your manuscript stays confidential throughout the project. We offer NDA protection for every project.
Can You Proofread My Book Before I Publish It on Amazon?
Yes. We can proofread your manuscript before you upload it to Amazon KDP and catch the errors you don't want readers to see first.
