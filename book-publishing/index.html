Frequently asked questions

How Long Does It Take To Publish A Book With Publishers Valley?

How Many Platforms Will My Book Be Available On?

Do I Keep The Rights And Royalties From My Book?

Yes. You maintain ownership rights to your work. We believe your story remains yours, which is why we provide clear agreements and sign an NDA to protect your confidentiality.

General Publishing Questions

Should I Publish My Book Myself Or Hire A Publishing Company?

It's up to your goals, budget, and the level of support you need. Some prefer to go solo; others go for a publishing company that can help better with professional editing and overall technicalities.

Do I Need An ISBN To Publish My Book?

Yes, an ISBN is important for the identification of your book and is often required for distribution through bookstores, retailers, and publishing platforms.

Can I Publish My Book In eBook And Paperback Formats?

Absolutely. Your book can be prepared for multiple formats, including eBook, paperback, and hardcover editions, depending on your publishing goals and distribution plans.

Do I Need An Editor Before Publishing My Book?

Yes. An editor helps fix mistakes and make your book a lot easier and more enjoyable for readers before it gets published.
