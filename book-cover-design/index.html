Book Covers By Genre

Handpick Your Genre and We'll Capture Its Visual Language

The cover is the main portal that gives readers access to the magical world you've painted with words inside. And every genre has its own vibe, or as we like to call it, the “visual language”, and readers expect to see it. Here are some of the main genres we specialize in:

Fantasy & Science Fiction Covers

The fantasy world has its own spell, and the sci-fi story has its own future. Our designers jump into each universe first, then build a cover with the right landscapes, mystical elements, and typography that instantly tells readers they've entered another world.

Romance Covers

Romance book covers aren't just about two people. It's about emotion. Doesn't matter if your story is sweet, contemporary, historical, or dark; our designers opt for the right colors and imagery to reflect the chemistry and feeling readers expect before they even open the book.

Mystery & Thriller Covers

A thriller cover should leave questions unanswered. That's exactly how we design them. Emphasizing bold typography and dark color palettes to hidden faces, isolated settings, and suspenseful visuals. Every element is carefully placed to spark curiosity without revealing too much.

Horror Covers

Fear comes in many forms, and every horror story commands its own atmosphere to be set. Be it any supernatural, psychological, gothic, or slasher story, our designers use haunting visuals, eerie contrasts, and unsettling details to create covers readers won't forget.

Children's & Educational Covers

Children notice colors before words. That's why our designers create the best children's book covers with bright, playful designs, easy-to-read typography, and engaging visuals. Just so they can reflexively connect with young readers, parents, and educators alike.

Self-Help & Non-Fiction Covers

A non-fiction cover should portray confidence at first glance. We keep the design clean and purposeful with meaningful visuals and color choices that communicate authority in order to make sure your message is impossible to miss.

Memoir, Biography & Autobiography Covers

Memoirs, biographies, and autobiographies are genres that should reflect your personal identity. We create covers with a clear focus on the author's name, capturing the exact enthusiasm of the story, allowing readers to connect with it on a personal level.

Poetry Covers

Poetry speaks through emotion, and your cover should do the same. We create dramatic and expressive designs with thoughtful artistic imagery. Using subtle color palettes that contextualize the mood and meaning behind every collection and word written inside.

Book Covers By Style

Multiple Artistic Directions for your Remarkable Story

Genre is one thing; it's just there to categorize your book. Style is what makes a reader pick your book from the shelf. Which is why we give authors the freedom to choose the exact style they envision for their book. Call it your own custom book cover.
