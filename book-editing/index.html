Editing Philosophy

We Strengthen Your Story Without Taking It Over

Editing is never about replacing what an author intended to say or simply hunting for mistakes. It’s about making the point more powerful. We sharpen weak spots in the story while keeping the foundation of your writing intact.

    Intent comes first.Your style sheet keeps track of the rhythms and quirks that make your writing yours.

FAQs

Your Book Editing Questions, Answered

What type of editing does my book need?
Every manuscript is different, so we start by reading a sample of your pages and giving you a customized recommendation rather than selling a one-size-fits-all package you may not need.
What is the difference between developmental editing and copyediting?
Developmental editing addresses big-picture issues like plot, pacing, structure and character arcs. Copyediting works at the sentence level, fixing grammar, punctuation and consistency once the structure is settled.
What is the difference between line editing and proofreading?
Line editing sharpens the reading experience itself voice, flow and word choice. Proofreading is the final pass that catches typos, formatting slips and stray errors right before publication.
How much does book editing cost?
Pricing depends on your manuscript's length and the type of editing it needs. We offer clear per-word and package pricing, provided after a free sample review of your pages.
How long does it take to edit a book?
Turnaround depends on manuscript length and the editing required. We give you a clear timeline once we've reviewed your pages, with a free sample edit returned within 48 hours.
Can I get a sample edit before I commit?
Yes. Send us 1,000–2,000 words and we'll edit them for free, so you can see exactly how we work before committing to a full book editing package.
Will you change my writing style or voice?
No. Voice preservation is always the priority. We edit from a custom style sheet built around how you write, not a generic template, and every change is tracked so you can see exactly what was adjusted and why.
Is my manuscript kept confidential?
Yes. Every project is covered by NDA protection from the moment you submit your manuscript, and you retain 100% ownership of your work throughout.
