Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect before you begin

Every project is different, but these answers cover the questions authors ask most often at the planning stage.

Ask Us Directly
Can I use only one publishing service?

Yes. You can request an individual service such as editing, cover design, or formatting, or ask us to coordinate multiple stages.

Will I be able to review the work?

Yes. Your plan includes review points so you can approve creative and production decisions before final files are completed.

Can you work with an unfinished manuscript?

We can begin by reviewing its stage and recommending whether development, editing, or another service should come first.

Can my book be prepared for print and ebook?

Yes. Formatting and cover requirements can be prepared for the editions selected in your project plan.

How do I receive a price?

Share your manuscript stage, word count, formats, and goals. We can then define the scope and provide a project-specific quote.
