What Can a Great Author Website Do for You?

A great author website carries all the information readers might be looking for in one place. Think about when we search for our favorite movie directors and actors after finishing a movie. Book readers are no different. When they could find everything about you in one place, a one-time reader could surely turn into a die-hard fan.

That's why your website needs a mobile-friendly interface and a smooth user journey that helps you connect directly with your audience. Having been in this field for more than 10 years, we have created author websites that rank well on SERPs. With a team of the most skilled developers behind every project, we know how to turn your presence into an engaging website.

  • A prominent homepage that puts your latest release front and center
  • A clean book view with blurbs, reviews, and retailer links
  • A proficient author bio and media kit for press and features
  • Email integration to draw readers in and grow your mailing list
  • SEO-ready blog posting to keep your content locatable
  • Responsive development for every screen and every reader

FAQs

Your Questions, Answered

Here are the answers to the most common questions authors ask us.

What Should an Author Website Include?
Your website should have your books, your bio, and social links. But we can also add your blog, email signup, and more as per your demand.
How Much Does an Author Website Cost?
Our packages start at $349. The final cost depends on the pages, features, platform, and level of customization you may need.
How Long Does It Take to Build an Author Website?
It depends entirely on the size of your website and the features you choose to work with. If you need it sooner, we also offer rush delivery options.
Which Platform Is Best for an Author Website?
It can be decided by looking at how you plan to use your website. We work with WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and custom code to help you choose the right fit.
Can I Update My Website Myself After Launch?
Indeed. We can make it easy for you to update books and other content yourself. Or you can let our team handle it for you.
Will My Author Website Be Mobile-Friendly?
Yes. Every website we design is built to work smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktops.
Can You Optimize My Author Website for SEO?
Yes. We optimize your website structure, content, and other key areas to help search engines find and understand your site.
Can I Sell Books Directly From My Author Website?
Sure. We can set up direct sales or connect your website to major retail platforms so readers can easily buy your books.
