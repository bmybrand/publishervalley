Houston Publishing & Ghostwriting FAQ

How long does publishing a book take in Houston?

Most Houston authors move from first consultation to a published, distributed book in three to six months, depending on manuscript length, editing rounds, and whether you're starting from an outline or a finished draft. Ghostwritten projects typically run four to eight months to allow time for interviews and drafting.

Do you offer ghostwriting services in Houston, TX?

Yes. We provide professional ghostwriting services for Houston authors across memoir, business, fiction, nonfiction, self-help, and Christian books, working by phone, video call, or in person with clients throughout the Houston area.

Can you help me self-publish a book in Texas?

Yes. We handle manuscript formatting, ISBN assignment, cover design, and distribution setup on Amazon KDP, Barnes & Noble, Apple Books, and 30+ retail platforms, while you keep full ownership and royalties.

How much do ghostwriting services cost in Houston?

Pricing depends on book length, research and interview time, and genre. During your free consultation, we review your project scope and provide a fixed quote so there are no surprise costs partway through your book.

Do I keep the rights to my ghostwritten book?

Yes. You retain 100% of the authorship, rights, and royalties for anything we write or publish on your behalf. Our ghostwriters work under confidentiality agreements and never claim credit for your book.

How do I promote a self-published book after launch?

Our book marketing team builds a launch plan around your genre and audience reader outreach, social campaigns, and Amazon optimization so your book keeps finding readers well past release week.
