Illustration Styles

Find the Look Your Story Was Written For

Before a character gets a face or a page gets its colors, there’s a bigger decision to make. Should the children's book drawing feel soft or dreamy? Strange or textured? We help match the illustration styles to the story.

Watercolor & Gouache

Blended colors with visible brushwork to give the pages a gentle storybook feel.

Best for: Quiet nights and tender stories

Flat Digital & Vector

Comes with bold shapes and strong colors that make scenes easy to follow.

Best for: Concept books and Modern early readers

Ink & Line

Hand-drawn marks can make a book feel loose, funny, and more personal.

Best for: Lessons and quirky characters

Mixed-Media Collage

Paint and digital elements all come together to create something less predictable.

Best for: Dreamy worlds and folk tales

Whimsical Cartoon

Exaggerated features and playful shapes give characters a chance to be funny.

Best for: Funny stories and Character-led adventures

Painterly & Realistic

Richer lighting and realistic environments that build a more immersive world.

Best for: True stories and meaningful themes

FAQs

Questions Before the First Sketch

How Much Does a Children’s Book Illustration Cost?

+

There’s no one price for every book. The style, number of spreads, and amount of artwork all change the final cost.

How Long Does It Take to Illustrate a Children’s Book?

+

A full book might take several weeks till you get the final art. More pages and detailed artwork naturally take longer.

What Illustration Styles Can I Choose From?

+

Watercolor, gouache, collage, and more. The style should always feel like it belongs to your story. If you are confused, we can also help you pick the right one.

How Many Illustrations Does My Picture Book Need?

+

That depends on the story. We look at the manuscript and page turns to figure out where the pictures need to do the main job.

How Many Revisions Are Included?

+

Two revision rounds are included per spread in the full package. Enough room to catch what feels wrong before the artwork is locked.

Will I Own the Rights to My Illustrations?

+

Absolutely. The artwork is made for your book, and the rights are included. Once it’s yours, you can use it where your publishing journey takes you.

Can You Illustrate My Book From an Existing Manuscript?

+

Yes. We’re a company that illustrates and publishes children’s books. Send us your story, and we’ll take it from there.
